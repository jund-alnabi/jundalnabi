About our reporting

Editorial Standards

Jundal Nabi publishes summaries of public cybersecurity reporting and online claims. A claim is not treated as independently verified fact unless the article says how it was verified.

Sources and attribution

Stories should identify their original source, link to a reliable public report where available, and distinguish first-hand confirmation from information reported by others. Do not present an unattributed social post as independently confirmed reporting.

Accuracy and context

Headlines and summaries should match the evidence and avoid overstating an allegation. Explain relevant uncertainty, dates, and context. Update a story when material facts change and make significant corrections clear to readers.

Privacy and safety

Do not publish passwords, access tokens, private personal records, or images that expose them. Redact sensitive details from screenshots and avoid directing readers to leaked datasets or instructions for unauthorized access.

Authorship

Stories published on this site use the byline Admin, representing the site's publishing account. Admin is a site byline and does not identify an independently verified individual reporter. Account emails and Firebase identifiers are never displayed.

Corrections and contact

If a story contains an error or sensitive material, contact the publisher using the site's listed contact channel. We will review the report and correct or remove material when warranted.
