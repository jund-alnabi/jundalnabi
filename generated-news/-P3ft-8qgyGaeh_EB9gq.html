Data Breach Alert: Threat Actor "JundAlNabi" Claims Leak of 22.5k E-Commerce Order Records

By Admin 10/11/2026 Public investigation

Threat actor JundAlNabi has posted claims regarding a data breach involving roughly 22.5k e-commerce customer order records. The alleged leak includes order tracking statuses, customer contact and shipping details, transaction identifiers, and marketing attribution data.

A threat actor operating under the alias JundAlNabi claims to have published a database containing approximately 22,500 e-commerce order records. Exposed Information The compromised dataset allegedly contains a combination of transactional data, customer PII, payment metadata, and technical logs, including:Customer PII: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and full billing/shipping physical addresses. Order Details: Order IDs, timestamps, purchase amounts, itemized product breakdowns, and order status tracking. Payment & Analytics Data: Payment processor method details (e.g., Stripe, PayPal), gateway transaction IDs, and customer IP/attribution metrics. Security Advisory Organizations and consumers are advised to remain vigilant against potential follow-on phishing attacks, social engineering, or targeted scams exploiting exposed contact and order details. Disclaimer: This alert is strictly intended for public awareness, risk mitigation, and security research purposes. We do not promote, share, or host leaked datasets or stolen data.
