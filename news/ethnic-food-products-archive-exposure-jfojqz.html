Ethnic Food Products Archive Exposure

By Admin October 10, 2026 Public investigation

A large archive of operational and financial documents surfaced publicly, highlighting the wider need for stronger digital accountability and hygiene.

Archive of 136 business files (PDFs, images, Excel, Word, PPT) from Ethnic Food Products Solution Ltd (Maharashtra), a fruit pulp and juice concentrate supplier. Contains invoices, POs, quality/registration certs (GST, FSSAI, PAN), tenders, quotes, sales sheets, and photos.
